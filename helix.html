CURRENT MODEL

Helix 0.1

Research Preview
~822K Parameters
342K Training tokens
2,000 Training steps
PyTorch Training stack

The first complete Helix experiment.

Helix 0.1 was trained from randomly initialized weights on a small experimental dataset.

The model demonstrates that Core AI can tokenize training data, train a transformer, save model checkpoints, and generate text through a complete model-development pipeline.

Its small dataset means it can memorize patterns from training data and should not be treated as a general-purpose language model. The purpose of Helix 0.1 is infrastructure validation.
