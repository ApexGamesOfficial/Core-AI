What Helix 0.1 proves

Helix 0.1 was trained from randomly initialized weights on a small experimental dataset. It demonstrates the complete development loop: tokenize data, train a transformer, save checkpoints, and generate text.

Its tiny dataset means it can memorize training patterns and should not be treated as a general-purpose assistant. The goal of Helix 0.1 is infrastructure validation.
