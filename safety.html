SAFETY

Capability requires
understanding.

Core AI's approach to safety begins with understanding how our models behave, where they fail, and how those limitations change as our systems become more capable.

HELIX 0.1

Know the
limitations.

Helix 0.1 is a small experimental research model trained on a limited dataset. It is designed to validate Core AI's model-development pipeline, not to operate as a production assistant or provide reliable real-world information.

OUR APPROACH

Measure before
deployment.

01

Evaluation

Test model behavior and capability before deciding where a model should be used.

02

Limitations

Clearly document known weaknesses instead of presenting experimental systems as more capable or reliable than they are.

03

Safeguards

Develop appropriate controls and protections as future Helix models gain broader capabilities and deployment options.

DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLE

Safety evolves
with capability.

The safeguards required by a small research experiment are different from those required by a widely deployed model. Core AI intends to evaluate safety requirements alongside the capabilities of each future Helix release.

TRANSPARENCY

Say what the
system actually is.

Research previews should be described as research previews. Experimental results should be described as experimental. Limitations are part of understanding the system—not something to hide.
