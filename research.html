RESEARCH

Understand the system,
not just the output.

Our work focuses on the foundations required to build, train, evaluate, and improve language models.

01

Model Architecture

Exploring transformer architecture, attention, embeddings, context processing, and efficient model design.

02

Training Systems

Building training loops, GPU workflows, checkpointing, sampling systems, and reproducible experiments.

03

Data & Tokenization

Studying how dataset quality, diversity, tokenization, and validation affect what a model learns.

04

Evaluation

Developing ways to distinguish memorization from generalization and measure meaningful capability improvements.

APPROACH

Research through
iteration.

Each Helix experiment gives Core AI another piece of the system to measure and improve. Small experiments establish the pipeline before larger models demand larger datasets, longer training runs, and more compute.
