RESEARCH

Understand the system,
not just the output.

Core AI research focuses on the systems underneath language models: architecture, training, data, tokenization, and evaluation.

RESEARCH AREAS

01

Model Architecture

Exploring transformer architecture, model structure, parameter scaling, and the systems that determine how Helix processes information.

02

Training Systems

Developing the training loop, checkpointing, optimization, monitoring, and infrastructure required to train Helix models reliably.

03

Data & Tokenization

Studying how training data is prepared and how text is represented before it reaches the model.

04

Evaluation

Building ways to measure model behavior, generalization, limitations, and improvements across research iterations.

APPROACH

Research through
iteration.

Small experiments establish the pipeline first. Each result helps determine what should change before larger models, larger datasets, and more compute are introduced.

CURRENT WORK

From pipeline proof
to better models.

01

Dataset V2

Develop a larger and more varied experimental dataset for the next Helix training cycle.

02

Subword Tokenization

Move beyond Helix 0.1's character-level tokenizer toward a more capable subword representation.

03

Validation

Separate training performance from validation performance so future experiments can measure generalization more clearly.
